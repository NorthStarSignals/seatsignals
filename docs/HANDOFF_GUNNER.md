## 1. What SeatSignals actually does

Seven pillars, one product:

1. **Reservations & Floor** — bookings, waitlist, capacity, no-show prevention, table-side payment
2. **Inventory & Ops** — par levels, waste tracking, vendor relationships, recipe costing
3. **Staff & Payroll** — scheduling, time clock, tip pooling, performance tracking
4. **Marketing & CRM** — email/SMS campaigns, customer segments, win-back automation, dead-hour flash deals
5. **Loyalty & VIP** — points programs, rewards, gift cards, birthday automation
6. **Catering & Events** — lead capture, packages, corporate accounts (Pro only)
7. **Analytics & AI** — revenue forecasting, sentiment analysis, profitability per item, weather-aware predictions

The **differentiator** is the AI layer woven through all of it:
- **Signal IQ** — watches your business 24/7, surfaces actions ("table 12 hasn't turned in 90 min, comp them a dessert")
- **Ask AI** — natural language queries ("what was last Tuesday's busiest hour?")
- **AI Sentiment Responder** — generates tone-perfect replies to every Yelp/Google review in under 3 seconds, trained on the restaurant's own brand voice
- **Dead Hour Optimizer** — auto-fires SMS/email flash deals during slow periods. Average 23% revenue lift in dead zones.

No direct competitor owns all seven pillars plus the AI layer. Toast owns POS. OpenTable owns reservations. SevenRooms owns upscale CRM. Nobody owns the operating layer above them — that's our wedge.

## 4. The demo flow (30 minutes)

This is the call structure. Memorize it.

### Minute 0-5 — Discovery
Don't demo yet. Ask:
- "Walk me through your morning. What tools are you in before noon?"
- "What's the last review you got that pissed you off?"
- "How do you decide what goes on the Tuesday night special?"
- "Who answers the phone when someone calls for catering?"

The answers tell you which pillar to lead with. If they're pissed about reviews → lead AI Sentiment Responder. If Tuesday is dead → lead Dead Hour Optimizer. If they complain about catering inquiries → lead Catering Lead Gen.

### Minute 5-7 — Sign them up live
- Go to **seatsignals.app** (on your screen, them watching)
- Click **Start free**
- Sign up with their real email (this creates a real account they keep)
- 6-step onboarding wizard — type their real restaurant name, address, cuisine
- Pick a brand voice (Warm, Professional, Upscale, etc.)
- Tell it their dead hours (Tue 2-5pm, Wed 2-5pm, whatever)
- Click **Start with free plan**

They now have a live Starter account on their own domain-less email. This is their actual account. Don't throw it away.

### Minute 7-9 — Load Demo Data
- In the dashboard header, top-right: click **"Load Demo Data"**
- 75 customers, 220 visits, 12 reviews, 5 catering leads populate instantly
- Now the dashboard has substance to show

### Minute 9-22 — The Tour
Click **"Take the Tour"** (floating red button bottom-right). The 30-step guided walkthrough hits every major feature. You don't have to do all 30 — the first 8-10 get the point across:

1. **Overview** — "Your entire restaurant on one screen"
2. **Signal IQ** — "AI watches 24/7 for things you'd miss"
3. **Ask AI** — TYPE A REAL QUESTION like "what's my 5-star review rate?" — let them see the response come back. This is the "holy shit" moment.
4. **Reviews inbox** — show the 12 reviews, mix of Yelp/Google/TripAdvisor
5. **AI Sentiment Responder** — this is your ace. Click on the 2-star review ("Waited 45 minutes despite reservation"). Click **Generate Response**. Out comes a perfect apologetic, on-brand reply in 2 seconds. Copy it. Tell them: *"This response would've taken your manager 20 minutes to write. You got it in 2 seconds, and it matches your brand voice. Do this 30 times a month and tell me what it's worth."*
6. **Dead Hour Optimizer** — show the Tuesday 2-5 rule, the 23% revenue lift chart. "This auto-fires flash deals when you're slow."
7. **Customers** — 75 contacts, each one enriched
8. **Pricing** (/pricing) — land the $1,044 → $299 math

### Minute 22-28 — The close
- "What would make this a no-brainer for you to try for 14 days?"
- Listen. Most operators will say something like "if it integrates with Toast" or "if the reviews part really works"
- Handle the objection. Then:
- "You're already signed up on Starter. I'm going to bump you to Growth right now for 14 days — no credit card. By day 12, you'll either be a customer or you'll have seen enough to know it's not for you."
- Get them to commit to a **15-minute check-in on day 7** and a **decision call on day 14**. Put it in the calendar while you're on the phone.

### Minute 28-30 — Leave-behind
- Send them the signup link confirmation
- Send a one-pager PDF (Malik: we need to build this — add to backlog)
- Send your calendar link for the day 7 check-in

## 6. What works vs. what's scaffolded

Be honest with prospects. Don't oversell. This is critical for trust.

### ✅ Fully real today — sell this confidently
- Sign up, sign in, onboarding wizard (creates real account in our database)
- Restaurant settings + brand voice (saved per-user)
- Customer list (CRUD, real DB)
- Reviews inbox (real DB, integration with Yelp/Google requires a bit more setup per account)
- Overview dashboard metrics (reads from real data)
- AI Sentiment Responder (live Anthropic API — actually generates unique responses each time)
- Ask AI (live Anthropic, answers questions from the real data)
- QR-code customer capture (public URL they can put on table tents, real DB)
- Stripe billing for Growth / Pro (real Stripe checkout — they really pay us)

### ⚠️ Works in the browser but not yet cloud-synced
Most of the secondary CRUD pages (inventory items, vendor list, staff directory, events, goals, loyalty rewards, etc.) **save to the user's browser** but don't yet sync to our servers. This means:
- They work perfectly in a single-browser demo
- State persists across reloads on the same computer
- If they open the dashboard on a different device, they won't see yesterday's changes yet

**How to handle this in a demo:** don't draw attention to it. Those features look and feel real. If a prospect asks explicitly about multi-device sync, say: *"Cross-device sync on every surface rolls out over the next 90 days. Core stuff — reviews, customers, reservations, AI features — is already cloud-synced. The extended ops modules are being migrated now."* That's the truth.

If a prospect's main use case IS the extended ops (e.g., they want to issue POs from 5 devices), flag it to Malik — we'll prioritize that module for cloud sync.

### 🚫 Don't promise these yet
- **Toast / Square POS integration** — we can connect at signup (manual upload) but real-time sync isn't live yet
- **Twilio SMS at scale** — works for test sends, haven't stress-tested above 1000/day
- **Custom POS integrations** — anything besides the big three requires a custom integration ($5K-$15K setup, Enterprise tier)
- **White-label / reseller** — not built, don't pitch
